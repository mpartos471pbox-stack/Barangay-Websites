Our Community

Binanuahan is one of the 38 barangays that make up Lagonoy, and it has shown steady growth over the years, with its population rising by nearly 24% between the 2015 and 2020 censuses. Like many barangays in Lagonoy, our community values farming, fishing, and close-knit Bicolano traditions.
