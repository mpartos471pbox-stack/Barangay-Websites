About Us

Getting to know Barangay Binanuahan, its people, and its place within Lagonoy.

Overview

Barangay Binanuahan is a rural barangay located in the Municipality of Lagonoy, Province of Camarines Sur, within the Bicol Region (Region V) of the Philippines. It sits at approximately 13.74°N, 123.53°E, at an estimated elevation of 9.5 meters above sea level, and uses the postal code 4425.

Population & Growth

According to the 2020 Philippine Census, Binanuahan had a population of 2,288, making up about 4.03% of Lagonoy's total population and ranking it the 8th largest of the municipality's 38 barangays. The barangay grew steadily from 738 residents in 1990 to 2,288 in 2020 — a rise of over 1,500 people in thirty years, including a notable +23.8% increase between 2015 and 2020 alone.

Local Governance

Like other barangays in Lagonoy, Binanuahan is governed by a Punong Barangay (Barangay Captain) together with the Sangguniang Barangay (barangay council) and the Sangguniang Kabataan (youth council), all elected during the synchronized Barangay and SK Elections. Way of Life

As a rural barangay, Binanuahan's residents are largely engaged in farming, fishing, and small-scale trade — livelihoods common across Lagonoy and the wider Bicol Region. The community continues to grow while keeping close to its traditions, faith, and Bicolano hospitality.
